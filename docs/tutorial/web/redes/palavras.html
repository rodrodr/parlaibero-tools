How it is made. The nodes are the library's most characteristic words and expressions: its lexicon against the rest of the country's corpus (signed G², the 250 most over-used), without numbers and without the Snowball stopwords of the language (pt, BSD-3-Clause). Two terms are joined when they occur in the same intervention more often than chance (G² ≥ 10.83) and the edge is among the 10 strongest of either term; its width is the association strength, observed / expected. Themes are Leiden communities of three terms or more. Multi-word expressions of the corpus are one node each.

Method. port of the Diarios Explorer's engine/coocurrencia.js (parlaibero-explorer). Differences: Reference counts come from every row of the country in the MCP's database (the explorer reads its FTS5 index of the same rows; the two tokenizers differ by about one token in a million). Speech only uses the explorer's fast segmentation (parenthetical stage directions and upper-case lines out) for any library; the explorer applies its full parser of the record below 25 million characters. Rows the corpus marks as not speech (dm_speech = 0) are not in the library's text. Displayed forms are the country's most frequent written form of each word.
