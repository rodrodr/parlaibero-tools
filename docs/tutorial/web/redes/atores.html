How to read it. co = interventions where both entities appear (volume). NPMI = how much more than chance they appear together, from −1 to 1 (affinity); thresholds use the unrounded value. Read them separately: a large co with a low NPMI is two frequent entities, not an association. Bias = (n₁/N₁ − n₂/N₂)/(n₁/N₁ + n₂/N₂): positive leans to esquerda, negative to direita.
